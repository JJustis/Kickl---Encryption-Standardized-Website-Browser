Kickl Certificate Authority

Watches ownership certificates for jttp:// names. Nothing here is done by hand: the CA checks, seals and logs every certificate automatically, and the whole log is public.

Get your certificate sealed

  1. Buy a name at the BTC shop and download your ownership certificate (.kcert).
  2. Drop it below. The CA verifies the shop's signature, makes sure no other claim clashes, and writes it to its log.
  3. Download the sealed certificate. Import it in the Kickl Browser and your site shows VERIFIED OWNER to every visitor.
Drop your .kcert file here, or click to choose it

Your certificate contains a secret. It travels to this page over HTTPS only for sealing; the CA keeps just a hash of the secret, never the secret itself. The Kickl Browser can also do this for you with SEND TO CA.
