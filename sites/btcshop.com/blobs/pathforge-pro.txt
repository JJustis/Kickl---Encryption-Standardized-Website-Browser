PATHFORGE PRO — demo delivery
=============================

This text is the "file" behind the PathForge Pro product on the example BTC Shop page. It was sealed
inside the page when the page was published, and it only opened because you gave the page's access key.

In a real shop this would be the thing the buyer paid for: a ZIP, a PDF, a licence key, a download
link. The seller publishes it as a protected blob, then sends each buyer the access key once their
payment has arrived.

Nothing here is real. The shop pays a Bitcoin testnet address.
